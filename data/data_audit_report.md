## 7. Temporal Overlap Analysis

| Dataset | Coverage |
|---|---|
| ICRISAT | 1966 – 2017 |
| Govt APY Crop Profile | 2021 – 2026 |
| Govt APY District Data | 2024-25 only |
| IMD NetCDF Rainfall | 2015 – 2025 |

**ICRISAT ∩ IMD:** 2015 – 2017 (ICRISAT ends 2017; IMD starts 2015) → **3 years overlap**

**APY Crop Profile ∩ IMD:** 2021 – 2025 → **5 years overlap** (state-level only)

**APY District ∩ IMD:** 2024-25 only → **1 year** (too limited for supervised ML)

**ICRISAT ∩ APY:** No overlap — ICRISAT ends 2017; APY starts 2021

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The primary supervised ML training source will be **ICRISAT** (1966–2017).
> IMD rainfall (2015–2025) overlaps with only the last 3 ICRISAT years.
> For the initial model, ICRISAT yield data will be used without IMD features.
> IMD features will be incorporated when historical rainfall data covering
> 1966–2017 is available, or when ICRISAT is replaced with a multi-decade dataset.

## 9. Potential Target Variable

- **Yield** (Kg per ha) is consistently available in ICRISAT for every crop × district × year
- No pre-existing `crop_failure` binary label exists in any dataset
- Initial supervised learning problem: **Yield Regression**
- Crop loss / yield deviation can be derived from predicted vs historical expected yield
